We’ve updated the Relay website to show more of the workflows we’re designing for community staff. This changelog covers the **website preview**, rather than a release of the connected app.

## Discord command preview

The animation now follows a moderator through two commands:

```text
/ban chrxs_dev abuse of vehicle 7d
/modlogs chrxs_dev
```

The username, reason and duration are shown as separate fields. Relay confirms the example ban, then displays a moderation log with the responsible moderator, reason and duration.

## Sessions across both platforms

The training preview now shows matching **Roblox Events** and **Discord Events** listings. Hosting, attendance and confirmed training results sit alongside the shared schedule.

## Clearer case histories

Each action in the example case timeline identifies the responsible moderator. Requested actions, confirmed results and appeal decisions remain distinct.

## Staff and shifts

A new staff preview brings together clocking in and out, breaks, timesheets, weekly activity targets and approved time off.
